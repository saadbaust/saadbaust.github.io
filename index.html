Research Interests: Image Processing, Computer Vision, & Deep Learning.

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), and an MSc student in CSE. My research is in computer vision and deep learning, where I build and evaluate image-processing models for visual recognition and analysis. I am most interested in methods that stay accurate and robust on real-world data rather than only on benchmarks, and in keeping that work reproducible and practical to deploy. I am actively publishing in this area and am looking for PhD opportunities to take it further.
